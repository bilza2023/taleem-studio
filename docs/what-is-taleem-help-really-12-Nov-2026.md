
# What is Taleem.help, really? (v2)

*Revised review: positioning, scope and decisions. 12 November 2026.*
*Supersedes the 11 October 2026 version. Section 0 lists what changed.*

---

## The crux

**Taleem.help is Pakistan's Tuition App: every question in your book, explained and answered.**

- **What it is (the category):** an app, not a video collection. A video collection competes with free YouTube and loses. An app competes with the tuition teacher, and that comparison we can win.
- **Why this one (the proof):** concepts are already free online. Numericals are where students get stuck, and nobody has built a lesson for every textbook question. That gap is why tuition exists.
- **What keeps it ahead (the moat):** every student question is answered under the lesson it belongs to, and good answers stay there for every future student.

The category gets attention. The proof makes the sale. The moat keeps us ahead. We need all three, and the pitch must not drop any of them.

## 1. What Taleem.help is

- **An app, sold as an app.** It's a web app: you open taleem.help, with no download and no store listing. The call to action is always the URL.
- **Subscribe to your book, for 1 year.** The flow is: subscribe to your book → video lessons & more → ask the teacher → your record.
- **One question, one lesson.** Students use it like a key-book: stuck on Q7, open Q7. Each lesson is short, light on data and easy to rewatch.
- **A lecture is an event; a lesson is an asset.** A live teacher's quality resets every day. Ours keeps improving, because every fix is permanent.
- **A learning space, not social media.** No feed, no gossip, no politics.

**Test for every headline, page and promo:** if it sells "videos" or "video courses", it's off-strategy. If it sells only "an app" without saying *every question in your book*, it's missing its proof.

## 2. Brand and messaging

- **Brand line:** Taleem.help — Pakistan's Tuition App
  - "Tuition" is the job parents already understand and pay for.
  - "App" means self-paced and on your phone, with no expectation of live teachers.
  - "Pakistan's" because the lessons are narrated in Urdu and English.
- **Proof line (restored):** *Every question in your book, explained and answered.*
- **Scope line:** Federal Board · New curriculum · Urdu + English · *(list only the classes and subjects that are actually complete)*
- **Taglines:** Aapki tuition, aapki jaib mein · Samajh na aaye? Pooch lo. · Jab chahein, jitni baar chahein · Class miss hone ki tension khatam · Har sawal, sab ke liye
- **Rules:** no "AI" in the messaging; no imagery of a big institute or campus; don't attack competitors; keep Pakistan in the message.
- **Rule on promises (new):** advertise only what's finished. Don't replace "Federal" with "School" until other boards actually have content.

---

## 3. Pricing

- **Base offer: 1 Book · 1 Year.** The hero banner is built around this. Annual only.
- **Price level: open.** It's between Rs 5,000 and Rs 8,000 per subject per year, so set the number on purpose but keep the plan simple. Every option is still a fraction of a year of tuition (WiseTutorHub charges Rs 7,000–10,000 per subject per month).
- **Campus (Tuition + App):** charged separately and priced higher. It includes the app for 1 year.
- **Bundles:** a Math + Physics package (around Rs 10,000) is fine. It's still one-year books, not a new plan type.
- **Not doing:** monthly plans, 6-month plans, or a separate monthly fee for Q&A. If the numbers don't work, raise the base price instead of taking pieces away.
- **Unit economics to keep in mind:** each extra employee costs roughly the revenue of 100 app students. Each subject must be profitable on its own.

## 4. Scope, in order

The October rule was *one board, one book, every question*. Competitors are shallow because their business models reward breadth. We grow in depth first and only then outward.

| Order | Scope | Condition to move on |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** | FBISE Math, Class 9 (plus 8 and 10 as their content is ready) | Every chapter finished before a batch reaches it |
| **Next** | FBISE Physics | Math books complete; Physics profitable on its own |
| **Later** | Other boards (e.g. Punjab) with the same course | FBISE Math + Physics complete and selling |
| **Later still** | Teacher-partnership courses (e.g. O-level English) and teacher-run courses (statistics, safety officer) on the platform | Proven demand (e.g. 20 students waiting); separate fee structure |

**Government-board books stay Taleem's own.** Teacher-run courses are a separate tier and must not dilute "every question in your book."

## 5. Go-to-market: incubation first

- **Strategy B first, then A,** with checkpoints along the way.
  - **B (now):** a Business Development Officer at Rs 30–40k plus a sales bonus, on a 3-month contract that then becomes permanent. The role covers promotion and running the pilot campus class, with soft targets. Higher pay can be tied to keeping a minimum number of campus students.
  - **A (later):** an experienced salesperson with hard targets.
- **Incubation lasts 1–6 months.** The campus class runs entirely on taleem.help, shown on a TV. No content is taught outside the app.
- **Campus students** fund the hire, give feedback on lessons, seed the first Q&A, and refer friends (with a gift for signing up a friend).
- **Accounts:** the admin creates them for admitted students, so capacity is limited by room size until the online opening.
- **Target:** about 300 students in year one if we work very hard, otherwise in about 1.5 years.
- **Belief:** paying students exist. The real risks are not having the subject they want and failing to strike profitable deals with the people we work with.

---

## 6. Q&A: the teacher is AI, reviewed by a person

- **Q&A is core, not an upsell.** "Every question gets answered" is half of the crux. If Q&A goes behind a paywall, few students will ask, and the question bank, which is our moat, never builds up.
- **How it works:** the AI answers right away, grounded in that question's own slides and narration. It uses the same method and notation the student just watched.
- **A wrong answer, once pinned, spreads.** Nothing becomes a pinned FAQ until the author has reviewed it.
- **Being honest with parents:**
  - Avoid: "Teachers answer questions." This implies a person writes each reply.
  - Use: *"Ask the Teacher: questions answered quickly, and every published answer is checked by the teacher."*
- **Free AI photo-solvers are the real competitor for answers.** What they can't give: answers that match our lesson, reviewed answers, the FAQs collected under each question, and narrated lessons on the current book.
- **Open decision:** do AI answers appear instantly (marked "unreviewed") or wait until reviewed?

---

## 7. The feedback loop: still our strongest point

**Questions + usage → revision → better lesson → fewer questions.**

| What a teacher does | In Taleem |
|---|---|
| Answers "I'm stuck here" | Ask under the question |
| Doesn't repeat itself | Reviewed answers pinned as FAQs |
| Notices where the class struggles | Usage data per question: views, rewatches, drop-offs |
| Improves the lesson | Revision queue driven by questions and usage |
| Tells the class what changed | "Updated" marks on lessons |

The loop lives on our own platform. **Data the first campus batch doesn't record is lost forever,** so tracking must be live before the batch starts.

---

## 8. Market (unchanged since October)

- **Sabaq.pk** made concepts into videos, organised by topic, not by textbook question. Its exercise solutions are text PDFs only.
- **Funded apps moved away:** Edkasa went to live and school classes, Maqsad to entry-test prep.
- **YouTube** gives one long video per exercise, so you need timestamps to find a sub-question.
- **The gap is structural.** Their business models reward breadth and watch time, not depth. They won't change course.
- **The moat:** about 1,500 reviewed, narrated presentations per book, plus a year or more of answered questions.

---

## 9. Locked principles (revised)

1. **Sell an app, not videos.** *(new)*
2. **The question is the unit.** Every question can be found and linked. This is the proof behind the app.
3. **Nothing goes live unreviewed,** whether lessons or pinned answers.
4. **Depth before breadth.** Finish one board and book before the next. Advertise only what's done.
5. **Q&A is core.** It's in the base price and builds the shared question bank.
6. **Never claim a human writes each reply.**
7. **Each subject must be profitable on its own.** *(new)*
8. **The hub is a personal dashboard plus course notices,** never a feed or chat.
